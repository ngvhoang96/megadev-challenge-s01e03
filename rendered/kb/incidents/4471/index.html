owner support deskknownreviewed 2026-11-05 · 0 days ago

Since April, people have reported wrong dates in bookings, invites, and reminders. By 4 October, the desk export held 412 unique reports. The export has 427 rows because 15 reports appear again in overlapping pages; count each report ID once.

The reports describe two separate problems, not one large parser bug. The shared parser accounts for 379 reports. A second problem in the booking form's date hint accounts for 33 reports. File and track them separately so each fix can be verified against its own reports.

Known as one merged ticket; split into two actionable issues. The parser reads dates month-first for day-first tenant locales. Separately, booking-v3 shows a month-first hint to day-first tenants; people follow that hint and the form interprets their entry incorrectly.

Lessons

  • A merged ticket can hide independent causes; classify by tenant, locale, form, and channel.
